LOCATION 

Category of personal information 

Geolocation – one-time current location of your phone or any other recipient/s, in case the latter has provided his explicit consent to share location.

Examples of Information Collected 

As described in our Terms of Use, our Services requires you to send an SMS/link (i.e., Find by Phone or Find by Link Services) to the recipient, and they will decide whether to share their current location with you. The Service is used entirely at your own responsibility to obtain the recipient’s consent. . 

Each received location is recorded in your profile and kept for a certain period of time (of max 1 year), where you will receive a notification about it. 

Collection Point and legal basis  

The geolocation of any detected phone will be collected at the moment the recipient clicks on the provided URL and allows their device to share the location. For security reasons and as additional data protection safeguards the Service is limited to (i) a certain number of attempts per day (when used with SMS) and to (ii) one-click-use only links, as these measures ensure that there will be no abuse of its use and are preventing a constant monitoring effect, which we do not offer as part of our Services. The legal basis for this processing of personal information is the consent of the recipient. The obtaining of such consent is the sole responsibility of the user.

We take responsible measures to avoid involuntary location sharing. In addition to all the purely technical and organizational measures we implement, we also emphasize, as we have described in detail in the Terms of Use, that it is your responsibility to send clear messages to the recipients of the SMS/link, ensuring they are not anonymous and clearly explain, as truthfully as possible, why you want to receive the location of a given recipient. We monitor for improper use of our services. With your SMS, an automatically generated message (disclaimer) from us is always included, stating that by clicking the link, the recipient gives consent to share their location. This consent is not stored by us, as it is not our responsibility to obtain it (we become aware of it due to the act of location sharing). The automatically generated message from us is intended to help raise awareness for the recipient and ensure the legal use of our services. 

A specificity of the provided by the recipient consent (as an opt-in feature) is that once granted the one-time current location is shared with you. This means that such consent cannot be revoked as by the time it was provided there was a legitimate legal basis for processing and the Service was completed. If the recipient no longer wishes to send you their location, they should refrain from clicking on any future links sent, as well as check/amend the settings on their phone. Of course, both you and the recipient can always contact us with any questions or requests for data deletion.
